# Third-party 3D assets

The room uses third-party models rather than hand-built placeholder geometry.

- Furniture models (`desk`, `chairDesk`, `bookcaseOpen`, `books`, `plantSmall2`, `pottedPlant`, `lampRoundFloor`, `lampRoundTable`, `computerKeyboard`, `computerMouse`, `speakerSmall`, `radio`, `sideTable`, `loungeDesignSofa`, `loungeDesignChair`) come from Kenney's **Furniture Kit** and are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**: https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
- `electric-guitar.glb` is **Electric guitar** by Poly by Google, distributed by Poly Pizza under **Creative Commons Attribution**: https://poly.pizza/m/eoSib2-NN9Y
- `modern_arm_chair_01`, `desk_lamp_arm_01`, `potted_plant_01`, and the `poly_haven_studio_1k.hdr` lighting environment come from **Poly Haven** and are released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**: https://polyhaven.com/license

No ownership of these third-party assets is claimed.
